Privacy Policy

What we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

Your name, email address and password (stored securely by our login provider); the profile details you add, such as location, biography, photos, services and prices; your messages, bookings and reviews; anything you send through the Contact form; and, for tradespeople, the certificates, licences or ID photos you upload. Those files are stored privately, checked automatically by an AI service to see whether they are readable documents, and seen only by you and TradeLink staff.

How we use it

To run your account, show listings in Search, let customers and tradespeople contact each other, review listings, keep TradeLink safe, and answer your questions. We do not sell your personal information.

Who can see what

A tradesperson's approved public listing is visible to everyone. Your email address is never shown on a listing. A customer profile is private by default, and you can choose what tradespeople see. Messages are visible only to the two people in the chat.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with our database provider, Supabase. Your browser keeps a small login session so you stay signed in.

Your choices

You can edit your profile at any time, switch a profile to private, or ask us to delete your account and data through the Contact page.
